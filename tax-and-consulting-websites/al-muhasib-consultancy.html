Additional questions

Questions you want to know.

I already have an accountant, why do I need a tax consultant as well?

Tax work requires current knowledge of tax laws and filing deadlines. A tax consultant can provide specialist support for compliance and timely completion of tax obligations.

Can you handle my accounts and finances?

Yes. The firm explains that accounting is key to business progress while tax planning is vital for books, accounts and financial matters, and it provides both services.

What sort of things can you do for me?

Tax, corporate, audit, accounting and bookkeeping services are among the core areas provided.

What type of business registration is suitable for my business?

Options include sole proprietorship, partnership business firm, and private/public limited companies. The appropriate choice depends on partners, financial position and industry requirements.

Is tax planning the same as tax avoidance?

No. The firm distinguishes legal tax planning and compliance from illegal tax avoidance, with the goal of reducing unnecessary tax exposure while complying with applicable laws.

Can I discuss a tax-related task before starting?

Yes. The homepage invites clients to consult by email, phone, WhatsApp or live chat regarding income tax returns, sales tax filing, withholding tax filing or other tasks.
