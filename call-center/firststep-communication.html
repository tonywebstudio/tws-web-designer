Answers

Frequently asked questions

What is a live transfer?

A live transfer is a pre-qualified prospect connected to your sales team in real time over the phone. FirstStep screens and verifies each lead against your campaign filters, then transfers the call directly to a licensed agent or closer — so your team spends time selling, not prospecting.

Which industries does FirstStep Communications serve?

FirstStep specializes in U.S.-focused financial services and home-services campaigns: mortgage refinance, debt settlement, solar and home services, and final expense. We also provide inbound customer support, dedicated BPO teams, and digital services.

How does FirstStep stay TCPA and DNC compliant?

Every campaign runs on TCPA-compliant dynamic scripting with automated, real-time data scrubbing against active Do-Not-Call registries before dialing. Calls are recorded for verification and quality control, reducing regulatory risk for your brand.

Where is FirstStep Communications located?

FirstStep operates from a U.S. office in Kennewick, Washington and a delivery center in Rawalpindi, Pakistan, with 500+ seat capacity and 24/7 operations support across both locations.

How quickly can a campaign launch?

With filters, scripts, CRM posting, and QA scorecards prepared up front, most campaigns move from scoping to go-live rapidly. Our launch-support team handles setup so your first transfers start landing without a long ramp.

What campaign volume can FirstStep handle?

With 500+ seats and 24/7 operations, FirstStep supports high-volume programs. Recent monthly delivery has exceeded 8,000 combined live transfers across mortgage, debt, solar, and final-expense verticals.
